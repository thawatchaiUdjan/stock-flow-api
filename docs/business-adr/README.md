# Business decisions

This namespace is separate from technical `docs/adr/`. Imported decisions 0001–0009 retain their original numbers, status, dates and decision text. Only relative historical link destinations were pinned to the source commit. Existing `context ADR NNNN` citations resolve here.

Product decisions 0001–0004, 0006–0007 and 0009 remain in force. Workflow decisions 0005 and 0008 are historical authority for past confirmations. [0010](0010-api-owns-record-and-workflow.md) supersedes their workflow for the API only; their imported status lines remain evidence of the original record, not an instruction to merge or coordinate through context.

Unqualified ADR numbers in imported business text mean this namespace unless explicitly naming API/web technical decisions. New business decisions increment this directory's highest number; new technical decisions increment `docs/adr/` independently. See [domain guidance](../agents/domain.md) and [source provenance](../migrations/context-ownership.md).
